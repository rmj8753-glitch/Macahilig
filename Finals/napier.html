Tai O Fishing Village

Best for Fishing

Tai O Fishing Village, located on Lantau Island, is one of Hong Kong’s oldest and most culturally rich communities. Known for its traditional stilt houses built above tidal waters, it offers a rare glimpse into the city’s heritage as a fishing hub. Visitors can explore its charming narrow alleys, local markets, dried seafood stalls, and small temples, or take a boat ride to see stilt homes up close and possibly spot the endangered Chinese white dolphins. With its slow-paced atmosphere, scenic coastal views, and strong local culture, Tai O remains one of Hong Kong’s most authentic and picturesque destinations.

Planning Tips: Plan to visit in the late afternoon so you can explore the village, enjoy a boat ride, and stay for the stunning seaside sunset.
