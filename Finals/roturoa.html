Nan Lian Garden

Tranquil, elegant, scenic

Nan Lian Garden is a beautifully designed classical Chinese garden located in Diamond Hill, Hong Kong. Created in the elegant style of the Tang Dynasty, the garden features serene lotus ponds, traditional timber architecture, rock displays, and its iconic golden pavilion. Surrounded by lush greenery and carefully crafted pathways, it offers a calm escape from the city’s busy atmosphere, blending nature, history, and artistry in one tranquil space.

Nan Lian Garden is a peaceful Tang-style Chinese garden known for its golden pavilion, scenic bridges, and perfectly manicured landscapes.

Planning Tips: Visit early in the morning for a quieter and cooler experience, follow the designated pathways for the best views, and pair your trip with the nearby Chi Lin Nunnery for a complete cultural visit.
