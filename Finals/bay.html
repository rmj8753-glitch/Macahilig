Ocean Park. Marine Theme Park

Thrilling, marine, family-friendly

Ocean Park Hong Kong is one of the city’s largest and most beloved theme parks, combining thrilling rides, marine-life exhibits, and wildlife encounters across its expansive hillside and waterfront areas. Visitors can enjoy roller coasters, cable car rides with panoramic ocean views, and immersive zones like the Grand Aquarium, Polar Adventure, and Rainforest exhibits. The park also features giant pandas, penguins, sea lions, and a variety of educational programs focused on conservation. With its mix of excitement and nature, Ocean Park offers a full-day adventure suitable for families, thrill-seekers, and animal lovers alike.

Planning tip: Arrive early to avoid long lines, start with the popular rides on the Summit area, and use the Ocean Express train or cable car to save time moving between zones.
