Tai Kwun

Best for Arts

Tai Kwun is the revitalized Central Police Station Compound in Hong Kong’s Central district, bringing together the former police station, magistracy, and Victoria Prison. The entire site has been transformed into a major cultural and heritage centre, featuring interactive museums, contemporary art exhibitions, historic colonial architecture, and a wide range of restaurants and shops, all within its beautifully restored open courtyard spaces.

Planning tip: Plan to spend at least 1–2 hours exploring the museums and exhibitions, visit early to avoid crowds, and check the event schedule ahead of time because Tai Kwun often hosts rotating art shows and cultural performances.
